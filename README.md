# habit_tracker
HABIT TRACKER (standalone) 1.0

How to Use It

Just open index.html on your computer
		-Works in any browser
		-Open link in browser of choice
			-save to homepage on mobile for ease of use.

Your data
  Habits are saved in the browser on the device you use. Different devices do not
  sync. Adding habits will not update on other user's devices.Use "Export backup" on the Today page to save a file, and "Import backup"
  on another device to load it. Back up now and then, because clearing browser
  data erases your habits.
